About

Simona Coltello (1998, She/They) is a curator and creative director of Pessima.

She has curated 20+ exhibitions and events since 2020, most recently producing 4K: Touching Grass in Ultra HD, a collective exhibit that reflected on artificial means of utopian life featuring international artists such as Eva & Franco Mattes, Alfred Francis Pietroni, Jenia Filatova and many more.
