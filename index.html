SIMONA COLTELLO

Graphic Design • Photography • Web Developer • Art Direction • Space Curation • Editorial Design
Simona Coltello (1998, She/They) is a curator and creative director of Pessima, "Trans Art* Magazine & Non Profit Association" dedicated to explore and represent Trans* identities. Her research is deeply influenced by online aesthetics, often blending Post-Internet and Post-Human elements.

About

Her works predominantly depict Trans identities* in metaphorical terms, frequently portraying dysmorphia, biomechanics, and monstrosity as empowering traits of an evolving body. She has curated 20+ exhibitions and events since 2020, most recently producing 4K: Touching Grass in Ultra HD, a collective exhibit that reflected on artificial means of utopian life. Digital Art, Early AI and 3D sculpture were the main focuses to explore dystopian imaginaries and the promethean corruption of the artists' depiction of beauty, featuring international artists such as Eva & Franco Mattes, Alfred Francis Pietroni, Jenia Filatova and many more.
